## Full description (4000 max)

```
Notes organized the way you think: a shelf of collections, books and pages. No account, no cloud, no ads. Everything stays on your phone.

ORGANIZE
• Collections hold books and loose notes, like a Recipes collection with a book for weeknight dinners and another for baking
• Books hold pages in the order you choose. Drag to reorder
• Loose notes for everything that doesn't need a book
• Colors, favorites and pinning to keep what matters on top
• Move anything anywhere: a note into a book, a book into a collection

WRITE
• A clean editor with titles, headings and subheadings
• Bulleted lists, numbered lists and checklists, with indenting
• Bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, highlight, quotes, inline code and links
• Saves as you type

FIND
• Search every title and every line you've written
• Sort by last updated, date created or name

PRIVATE
• Lock the whole app, or just the collections, books and notes you choose
• PIN or password, with fingerprint or face unlock
• Locked items never show up in search or previews
• Your notes never leave your phone unless you export them

SAFE
• Recently deleted keeps anything you delete for 30 days
• Undo right after a delete
• Back up to a file, protected with a passphrase if you like, and restore it on any phone
• Export everything as Markdown

Light and dark themes with seven accent colors.

Free, with no ads and no tracking.
```
