# Privacy Policy for PB Notes

_Last updated: November 16, 2026_

PB Notes is a note taking app made by Patrick Basham. This policy explains what happens to your
information when you use the app. The short version: **your notes stay on your phone, and we never
see them.**

## What the app stores

Everything you put into the app is saved only on your device:

- collections, books, pages and notes, including their titles, text, colors and order
- which items are favorites, pinned or locked
- items in Recently deleted, until they are removed for good after 30 days
- app settings such as appearance and sort order

If you set a PIN or password, it is never stored. The app keeps only a salted, one way hash of it in
your phone's secure storage (Android Keystore), which is used to check what you type. Fingerprint or
face unlock is handled by your phone; the app only learns whether it succeeded.

## What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account, no sign in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, no
in-app purchases and no servers. It does not connect to the internet to send or receive your notes.
We cannot read, recover or delete your notes because we never have them.

## Backups and exports

Your notes can leave your phone only in these ways, and never to us:

- **Your phone's own backup:** if Google backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may
  include the app's data in your Google account backup, as it does for other apps. That copy belongs
  to your Google account; we can't see it. It never includes your PIN or password.
- **Backup file:** a file with everything, made in the app and sent wherever you choose with your
  phone's share sheet. You can protect it with a passphrase, which encrypts it with AES 256. Without
  the passphrase it cannot be opened, including by us. Backups include locked items.
- **Markdown export:** a readable text copy of your notes, saved the same way. It is not encrypted.
  Locked items are listed by name only.

Once you share a file, it is handled by the place you saved it to, under that service's own privacy
policy.

## Permissions

- **Biometrics:** only used if you turn on fingerprint or face unlock for your locks.

The app does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

## Children

PB Notes is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Deleting your data

Delete items in the app (they go to Recently deleted, which you can empty), or uninstall the app to
remove everything from your phone. Backup files you saved elsewhere are yours to delete.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
